3 Leading Indicators of Fatigue
● Spend Prioritization Decay: Budget share drops in 72h window.
● Front-End CPC Creep: Rising click costs while impressions lag.
● Concept Saturation: Declining marginal conversion efficiency.

The 3-Stage Signal-Led Testing Framework

Stage 1

Concept & Motivation Validation

Evaluates 4 user psychological motivations against 3 execution formats in dedicated testing ad sets.

Budget: ~10x target CPA per concept.
Kill Rule: Pause if >2x target CPA after 500+ impressions.
Stage 2

Structural Refinement

Optimizes structural pacing, clip duration (6s punch vs 30s walkthrough), and on-screen talent demographics.

Flight: 5–7 day controlled multivariate test.
Decision: Maximize downstream CVR efficiency.
Stage 3

Scale & Micro-Swaps

Graduates winning assets using original Post IDs to preserve social proof; deploys opening frame swaps to combat fatigue.

Execution: Micro-swaps injected at leading fatigue indicators.
Outcome: Extends winner lifespan by 3–5 weeks.

● Module 6 • Agency Implementation

Commercial Rollout & Client Transition Playbook

Transitioning an agency from open-ended billable hours to productized creative bundles requires a phased rollout to protect service margins and establish clear operational boundaries.

Phase 1 ● Internal Setup

Scope Boundaries & Capacity

Define exact output boundaries to eliminate unbilled revisions. Cap Tier 2 at 2 core concepts and 15–20 variants. Align team capacity around shared digital asset management and frame-accurate review software.

Key Action: Transition creatives to 4-role pods and set rigid batch production deadlines.
Phase 2 ● Pilot Rollout

Land-and-Expand Strategy

Identify 1-2 existing growth-stage clients facing creative fatigue or rising CPAs. Deploy Tier 1 (30-day Audit & Sprint) to demonstrate isolated ROC improvements before proposing long-term Tier 2/3 subscriptions.

Key Action: Use Tier 1 empirical POC proof to convert clients into recurring monthly subscriptions.
Phase 3 ● Commercial Messaging

Repositioning Sales Pitches

Pitch creative as a measurable growth driver, leading with structured testing systems that mitigate the 1.6x CPC fatigue curve and capture the 75–95% decile spend share.

Key Action: Shift client check-ins from subjective aesthetic reviews to monthly motivation layer telemetry.
